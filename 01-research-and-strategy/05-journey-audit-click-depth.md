# Journey Audit: Spatial Canvas vs. Traditional Linear Tables

## Objective
Demonstrate how Synapse's infinite spatial canvas reduces navigation click-depth by **60%** when investigating cross-entity relationships across **People**, **Transactions**, and **Documents**.

## Task Scenario
*Find a connection between a suspect/research entity (Person), a specific record (Transaction), and supporting evidence (Document), then group them into a cluster.*

### 1. Traditional Linear Table Workflow (10 Clicks / Context Switches)
1. Open **People** database table.
2. Search and click to open **Person A** detail page.
3. Copy Person A's ID / reference code.
4. Click browser back / sidebar to switch to **Transactions** table.
5. Paste ID in filter bar and apply filter.
6. Click to open **Transaction #402** detail drawer.
7. Note linked reference document ID.
8. Switch view to **Documents** repository table.
9. Search and open **Document B** in a separate tab.
10. Switch to a separate notes/slides app to manually record the connection.

### 2. Synapse Spatial Canvas Workflow (4 Clicks — 60% Reduction)
1. **Pan/Zoom** directly to the entity cluster on the unified canvas (0 clicks; gesture-based).
2. **Click** `Person A` node to highlight all connected **Transaction** and **Document** nodes in place.
3. **Marquee-select** the highlighted `Person`, `Transaction`, and `Document` nodes together.
4. **Click `Synthesize Canvas`** to auto-group them and generate the relationship summary in the Right Context Inspector.

## Result
* **Traditional Tables:** 10 discrete clicks + 4 full page/tab switches.
* **Synapse Spatial Canvas:** 4 direct actions inside a single persistent viewport (**60% click-depth reduction**).
